Chapter 2: LANs & WANs in Action

  • Sketch (on paper) your home LAN and label: end devices, router, modem, ISP.
  • Explain how your LAN talks to your ISP's WAN step-by-step.
  • Describe a business scenario where multiple office LANs use a WAN.
  • Explain why WANs are usually slower and more complex than LANs.
Mini Quiz

Checkpoint: LAN vs WAN

Chapter 4: Protocol Arena

Chapter 5: Transmission & Media

Media Hunt

  • List 3 wired media and 3 wireless media and a typical use-case for each.

Quest Log

Chapter 6: Devices & Components

Device Roles

  • Describe what an end device is and give 3 examples.
  • Explain how intermediary devices (switch, router, access point, modem) help traffic move.
  • Describe what a NIC is and why the MAC address matters.
  • Compare a hub and a switch in terms of efficiency and OSI layer.

Software & Servers

  • Explain the purpose of network management software and give one example task it does.
  • Describe what a software firewall does on a single host.
  • Explain the role of a server in the client-server model when you load a web page.
  • List at least 3 types of servers (e.g. web, file, mail, database) and what each serves.
