Chapter 2: OSI Model – 7‑Layer Tower

Learning goal: Be able to name the 7 layers in order and give one concrete example for each.

Layer Cards

LayerNickname
7 – ApplicationWhat user apps use (HTTP, SMTP)
6 – PresentationTranslate, encrypt, compress
5 – SessionStart, keep, end conversations
4 – TransportReliable segments (TCP/UDP)
3 – NetworkIP addressing, routing
2 – Data LinkFrames, MAC addresses
1 – PhysicalBits on the wire / air

Chapter 5: Transmission Trials – Signals, Modes, Media

Learning goal: Distinguish analog vs digital, simplex/half‑duplex/full‑duplex, and compare common media.

Modes & Types

    >Analog: continuous signals (e.g., old radio). >Digital: bits (0/1) as pulses – what networks use. >Simplex: one‑way only (keyboard → PC). >Half‑duplex: two‑way, turn‑taking (walkie‑talkie). >Full‑duplex: two‑way, simultaneous (phone call).

Media Arena

MediumTypical Use
Twisted pairEthernet in LANs
CoaxialCable TV, older LANs
FiberBackbones, high‑speed WANs
RadioWi‑Fi, cellular
MicrowaveSatellite links
InfraredShort‑range remotes
